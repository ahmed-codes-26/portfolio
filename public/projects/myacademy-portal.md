# MyAcademy Portal

Ahmed Ali's full-stack academy management project, inspired by the practical responsibilities of running MyAcademy.

## Implementation

React frontend, Node.js and Express backend, MongoDB and Mongoose persistence, JWT authentication, role-based access, Cloudinary media storage, and WhatsApp-Web.js integration.

Admin workflows manage student and teacher accounts. Teachers manage attendance, fees, and academic resources. Students access their attendance, fee information, and class resources.
