# Konekt

A social networking application developed by Ahmed Ali during his completed remote web-development internship at Arch Technologies.

## Implementation

Node.js, Express, EJS, MongoDB, Mongoose, Socket.io, JWT, Multer, and bcrypt. Features include registration, email verification, account recovery, profile customization, image uploads, posts, likes, comments, friend requests, public/private profiles, and persistent notifications with real-time interactions.

Konekt connects authentication, data relationships, socket sessions, and persistence in one application.
